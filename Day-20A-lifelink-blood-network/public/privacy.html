Privacy Policy

Draft for product planning only. Not legal advice and not reviewed or approved by a Pakistani lawyer. Have qualified Pakistani counsel review it against actual hosting, messaging, email, and retention practices before launch.

Information collected

Depending on how you use the service, LifeLink may process your name, email address, phone number, city, donor profile information, account verification state, blood requests, offers, reports, profile photo, optional identity documents submitted for manual review, support-ticket category and message, administrator replies, and basic operational records.

How information is used

Information is used to operate accounts, verify contact channels, present relevant requests and donor profiles, coordinate accepted offers, protect users, investigate reports, and maintain platform security.

Sharing and visibility

Public request listings omit patient and requester names and expose only the fields needed for matching. Phone details are not part of public listings and are shared for an accepted offer only when neither party has blocked the other. Support tickets and administrator replies are private and visible only to the ticket owner and authorized administrators. Information may be disclosed when legally required or necessary to address a credible safety concern.

Security and retention

Optional identity documents are encrypted on the server and are available only to authorized administrators for manual review. They are not included in public profiles. You may withdraw identity verification and delete the uploaded document from Account safety. Account, report, and audit records may be retained as needed for service operations, safety, and legal obligations. Define and implement specific retention periods before production use.

Your choices

You may update profile details, verify your email or phone, and block or report accounts using available controls. Contact the service operator for access, correction, deletion, or privacy questions; applicable rights and response timelines require local legal review.

Third-party providers

Email and SMS/WhatsApp verification may be delivered through configured third-party providers. The support WhatsApp button opens a user-initiated chat link; LifeLink does not operate a WhatsApp support API. Their processing and any cross-border transfers depend on the provider and deployment configuration and must be disclosed accurately before launch.
