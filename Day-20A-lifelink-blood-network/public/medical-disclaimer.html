Medical disclaimer

LifeLink is not a hospital, blood bank, medical service, or emergency-response provider. This notice is a product draft and needs review by qualified Pakistani legal and medical professionals.

No medical advice or emergency response

Information on LifeLink is provided for coordination purposes only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a substitute for professional care. In an emergency, contact local emergency services or a licensed healthcare facility directly.

Professional assessment is essential

LifeLink's ABO/RhD red-cell compatibility result is an educational software-matching filter only, not medical approval. Actual transfusions require professional blood-bank testing and crossmatching. Only qualified healthcare professionals and authorized blood services can assess donor eligibility, blood typing, compatibility, screening, collection, storage, and transfusion. A profile, match, or offer on LifeLink does not establish medical suitability or safety.

No guarantee or endorsement

LifeLink does not verify every user statement and cannot guarantee that a request is accurate, a donor is available, a donation will occur, or a blood product is safe or appropriate. Users should confirm all arrangements through qualified healthcare professionals and facilities.
