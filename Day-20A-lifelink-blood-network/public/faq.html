Frequently asked questions

How do I register?

Create a donor or requester account, accept the site terms, and verify your email. Donors also provide their blood group.

Who may donate?

LifeLink checks account availability and the configured interval since the donor's last recorded donation. This is not a medical eligibility decision; a qualified healthcare professional must assess donation suitability.

How do blood groups and compatibility work?

LifeLink uses its configured blood-group matching rules to help identify possible donor matches. This does not guarantee medical compatibility; the receiving healthcare facility must confirm compatibility and suitability.

Why is there a donation interval?

The app uses its configured minimum gap between recorded donations to avoid suggesting donors before that interval. Follow advice from a qualified healthcare professional about when you may donate.

How do I create a blood request?

Sign in as a requester, enter the required blood group, units, hospital, city, urgency, and needed-by date, then post the request. Avoid putting private medical details in the optional note.

How do I submit an offer?

Sign in with an available donor account, open a current request, and submit an offer. The app checks configured compatibility and recorded eligibility before accepting the offer.

How do I accept or decline an offer?

The requester can review offers in their workspace and accept or decline a pending offer. Contact details are shown only to the involved participants after acceptance and when account blocks do not prevent sharing.

How is donation completion recorded?

Both the donor and requester must independently confirm completion for the offer to become Completed and update donation history. Ratings are tied to completed offers and are subject to moderation.

How do notifications work?

Notification channels depend on the user's preferences and the server's configured email or SMS/WhatsApp providers. Delivery is not guaranteed when a provider is unavailable or unconfigured.

Is identity verification required?

Email verification is required to sign in as a donor or requester. Phone verification and identity-document review are separate; identity-document review is optional, encrypted, and reviewed by an administrator.

How can I report or block someone?

Signed-in users can report a user or request and block/unblock another account. Administrators review reports. Do not use reports as an emergency response channel.

How do I contact support?

Use the support form, email daniyalkhaniii49@gmail.com, call +92 324 0128349, or message LifeLink on WhatsApp. Signed-in users can view their submitted tickets and replies on the Contact page. WhatsApp support is a chat link, not an automated WhatsApp API.

How is my information protected?

The public request board omits patient and requester names and contact details. Private account and identity data are not displayed publicly. See the Privacy Policy for more information.

Does LifeLink provide medical advice or emergency response?

No. LifeLink is a coordination platform, not a medical or emergency service. If someone's life is in immediate danger, contact your nearest hospital or call Pakistan Rescue 1122 immediately.
